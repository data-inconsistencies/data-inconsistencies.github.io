Method & contributing

Each N-way match is split into every pair of engines. For each pair the expected score is 1 / (1 + 10(Rb−Ra)/400); a win scores 1, a tie 0.5. An engine's change is K / (N−1) × Σ(actual − expected) with K = 32, so ratings stay zero-sum. The full spec is in docs/elo/spec.md.

The ClickBench column is for contrast: ClickBench is a single-table, scan-heavy benchmark, while most ELO matches are join-heavy TPC suites. It shows the product's best entry in ClickBench's default view: its rank among {{ site.Data.elo.clickbench.systems }} systems and its Combined score (×1.00 is the best system; lower is better). "—" means the product has no entry there; hover for why. Updated {{ site.Data.elo.clickbench.updated }}.

Got results? Publish them publicly, then add a match to data/elo/matches.json in a pull request, or email atwong@alumni.uci.edu. Backdated reports are welcome.
