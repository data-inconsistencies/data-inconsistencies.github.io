We use essential cookies to make this site work and, with your consent, analytics (Google Analytics) and marketing/CRM cookies (HubSpot, Brevo). Some pages include links or embedded content from Google Docs, YouTube, Calendly, or LinkedIn. If you interact with that content, those providers’ terms and cookies apply.

The [landscape](https://data-inconsistencies.github.io/landscape/) uses Google Analytics only after you allow analytics in the cookie banner. It records which items you open and which links to other sites you follow.

The [rankings](/rankings/) page shows comments from GitHub Discussions through [giscus](https://giscus.app). Opening that page loads content from giscus.app, and commenting requires signing in with GitHub. Comments are public and stored on GitHub. See the [giscus privacy policy](https://github.com/giscus/giscus/blob/main/PRIVACY-POLICY.md) and the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
