Benchmarks lie. But if we collect all of them, a clearer picture emerges. Each public
benchmark report (TPC-H, TPC-DS, SSB, …) is a **match**: sometimes 1-on-1, sometimes a
4- or 6-way battle. Every engine starts at **1500**, and the ratings update after each
match, oldest first. Older reports count for less: a match's weight halves every two years.
